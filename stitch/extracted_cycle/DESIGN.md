## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the intersection of Scandinavian reduction and warm, sensual intimacy. Created for couples synchronizing both emotional romance and biological rhythm, the visual language rejects cold clinical medicalism and vulgar explicitness. Instead, it creates an emotionally safe, sacred, and luxurious digital sanctuary.

Key characteristics:
- **Atmospheric Warmth:** Organic curves, quiet typography, and generous negative space make users feel secure, cared for, and deeply connected.
- **Sensory Tactility:** Subtle physical presence through fine 1px–1.5px hairline borders, pillow-soft container radiuses, and ambient, warm-tinted diffused shadows.
- **Poetic Clarity:** Complex biological information—such as ovulation windows and partner libido synchronization—is translated into fluid, glanceable indicators that feel like living art rather than clinical diagnostics.

## Colors

The palette is tuned around romantic depth, warmth, and skin-tone harmony, replacing sterile greys and blacks with deep wine and berry pigments.

### Core Roles
- **Primary (`#6B113B` - Deep Berry):** Serves as the grounded anchor. Used for primary branding touchpoints, emphasized headers, structural navigation icons, and active pill states.
- **Secondary / Action (`#C0264D` - Crimson Flame):** Conveys passion, vital pulse, and interactive focus. Used for primary CTA buttons, intimate check-in notifications, and high-priority action alerts.
- **Tertiary (`#E2847A` - Rose Gold):** Provides warm metallic glow and tenderness. Used for secondary highlights, badge outlines, celebration sparks, and timeline tracking markers.
- **Neutral / Text (`#2C0E28` - Rich Plum):** The foundational ink. Applied across all primary body copy, titles, and hairline outlines to prevent the harsh contrast of pitch black.

### Canvas & Surface
- **App Canvas:** `#FAF7F5` (Warm Alabaster Canvas), delivering an enveloping parchment foundation.
- **Elevated Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White) for crisp structural focus, paired with `#FDE8E9` (Soft Blush) for contextual companion cards, callouts, and intimate prompts.

### Cycle & Status Semantics
Biological cycle awareness uses a soft traffic-light harmony that retains clinical clarity within romantic softness:
- **Safe Window:** `#10B981` (Emerald Green) with `#E8F5E9` container fill.
- **Caution / Safety Buffer:** `#F59E0B` (Warm Amber) with `#FEF3C7` container fill.
- **Peak Fertile / Ovulation:** `#EF4444` (Coral Crimson) with `#FEE2E2` container fill.
- **Period / Menstruation:** `#8B5CF6` (Soft Lavender Violet) with `#EDE9FE` container fill.

## Typography

The typography centers on Outfit, a geometric sans-serif with natural circular balance and open apertures. It evokes modern editorial luxury while remaining readable on small mobile screens.

- **Headings & Display:** Kept tight and intentional. Negative letter-spacing in display sizes adds editorial presence to mood dashboards and daily greetings.
- **Body & Captions:** Ample line heights (1.5x–1.6x) ensure psychological breathing room and unhurried reading during private moments.
- **Numbers & Metrics:** When displaying cycle countdowns and date markers, Outfit's clean tabular numerals deliver clarity without shifting layout lines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built around mobile-first intimate interactions, paired with a dynamic fluid column architecture for larger displays.

- **Mobile Canvas (<640px):** Single-column layout bounded by a `margin-sm` (16px) or `margin` (20px) outer rim. Elements utilize horizontal snap-carousels for daily prompt cards and cycle stages, keeping vertical scroll fatigue low.
- **Tablet / Split View (640px–1024px):** 2-column dual-dashboard layout, presenting Partner A's biological/mood state on the left, Partner B's status on the right, and the mutual intimacy overlap in the center.
- **Desktop (>1024px):** 12-column layout centered with max width of 1180px, flanked by `margin-lg` (32px).
- **Rhythm:** Interior component padding relies consistently on `space-lg` (24px) for cards to establish an open, unhurried, Scandinavian airiness.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses warm, diffuse ambient layers rather than dark, detached shadows. Cards should feel like delicate stationery floating just above cashmere fabric.

- **Micro-Shadows:** Standard cards use a multi-tiered ambient shadow tinted with the base ink color:
  `box-shadow: 0 2px 4px rgba(44, 14, 40, 0.02), 0 8px 24px rgba(107, 17, 59, 0.05);`
- **Float & Active State:** Lifted surfaces (such as intimate mood sliders or active date-night cards) expand their diffusion:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 8px rgba(44, 14, 40, 0.03), 0 16px 36px rgba(107, 17, 59, 0.08);`
- **Hairline Tactility:** Every card pairs with an ultra-delicate 1px to 1.5px solid border in `#2C0E28` at 6% to 10% opacity, or `#E2847A` at 25% opacity for highlight containers.
- **Glass Transparency:** Floating navigation bars and cycle modal backdrops leverage 16px background blur over `#FAF7F5` at 85% opacity, preserving contextual warmth beneath overlays.

## Shapes

The design system embraces generous, organic, protective curvature to reject harsh mechanical angles and project warmth.

- **Signature Cards:** Use a uniform radius of 24px (`rounded-lg` equivalent in this high-radius scale), creating gentle pillow contours.
- **Interactive Triggers & Pills:** Action buttons, status badges, and cycle indicators use pill shapes (9999px) to invite touch.
- **Nested Inner Surfaces:** Nested status indicators or inset micro-cards scale down proportionally to 12px–16px radius to keep visual concentricity balanced.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Pill-shaped, Crimson Flame background (`#C0264D`), white text (`#FFFFFF`), with an inset 1px highlight border. On press, scales down slightly (0.98 scale) with deepening warm shadow.
- **Secondary Soft Action:** Soft Blush background (`#FDE8E9`), Deep Berry text (`#6B113B`), no shadow, 1px border at 10% Deep Berry opacity.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** Rich Plum text (`#2C0E28`) with underline decoration on active hover, zero border.

### Intimate Mood & Connection Chips
- Pill-shaped tokens (32px to 40px height) used for desire tagging, energy levels, and symptom logging.
- **Unselected:** Canvas-colored background (`#FFFFFF`), subtle hairline border (`#2C0E28` at 10%), Plum text.
- **Selected:** Soft Blush fill (`#FDE8E9`), 1.5px Rose Gold border (`#E2847A`), Deep Berry text (`#6B113B`).

### Cycle & Status Badges
- Compact indicators containing an 8px circular status pip alongside concise label copy.
- Composed using semantic tints: e.g., Ovulation Peak displays Coral Crimson text on a `#FEE2E2` pill background with a pulsing `#EF4444` core dot.

### Cards
- **Couple Sync Card:** 24px rounded white container with 1px border in `#2C0E28` at 6% opacity, filled with 24px interior padding (`space-lg`). Houses comparative bio-rhythms without visual clutter.
- **Reflection / Prompt Card:** Soft Blush fill (`#FDE8E9`), border in `#E2847A` at 20% opacity, featuring headline font in Deep Berry for sensual intimacy queries.

### Inputs & Date Controls
- **Fields:** Rounded-xl (16px), background in white, border in 1.5px `#2C0E28` at 12% opacity. Active focus shifts the border to Rose Gold (`#E2847A`) with a subtle 3px ring of `#FDE8E9`.
- **Toggle Switches:** Soft ovular pill track. Inactive in muted sand-tinted plum; active in Crimson Flame (`#C0264D`) with a white floating pebble thumb.

### Custom Component: The Dual-Sync Wheel
- A concentric dual-ring interaction widget placed on the home dashboard. The outer ring tracks cycle progression through soft gradient transitions; the inner ring displays partner desire alignment and emotional warmth. Both rings resolve at the apex to display sync status without clinical data overwhelm.
